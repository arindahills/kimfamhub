# KimFam Hub — Complete App Guide

## How to Navigate KimFam Hub

**On a phone (mobile):** The navigation is a tab bar at the BOTTOM of the screen. You will see: Home, Finances, Members, Projects, Ask KimFam, and More. Tap any of these to go to that section. There is NO left sidebar or hamburger menu on mobile.

**On a computer (desktop/tablet):** The navigation is a sidebar on the LEFT side of the screen. All sections are listed there.

---


## How to Log In
1. Open kimfamhub.com in your browser or on your phone.
2. Tap "Sign In" at the top right.
3. Enter your name exactly as registered (e.g. Hillary, Israel, Hellen, Max) and your password.
4. Tap "Sign In". If it is your first time, you will be prompted to change your password immediately.

## How to Change Your Password
1. After logging in, tap your name badge at the top right.
2. Tap "Account Settings".
3. Enter your current password, then your new password (at least 6 characters), then confirm it.
4. Tap "Change Password". You will see a green confirmation message when it succeeds.

## How to Reset a Forgotten Password
1. On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?".
2. Select your name from the dropdown list.
3. A 6-digit code will be sent to your WhatsApp number.
4. Enter the code and choose your new password.
5. Tap "Reset Password". You will be logged in automatically.
6. If the WhatsApp code does not arrive within 2 minutes, contact Hillary or Hellen.

## How to Replay the App Tour
If you want to replay the welcome walkthrough that explains each section of the app:
1. Go to "Ask KimFam" (tap Ask KimFam in the navigation).
2. Scroll down to the bottom of the Ask section.
3. Tap the "Replay App Tour" button.

## Finances Tab (💰)

### Viewing Club Finances
1. Tap "Finances" in the app.
2. At the top you will see the Club Summary: total contributions collected, total expenditure, expected balance, and the confirmed ABSA bank balance.
3. Below that is the Reconciliation indicator: green (in sync), yellow (small gap — likely bank charges), or red (large gap — check for missing records).

### Viewing Family Balances
1. In the Finances tab, scroll down to the "Family Balances" section.
2. Each family card shows:
   - Monthly: what they owe or how far ahead they are (green = paid up or in credit, red = arrears)
   - Opening Balance: how much of the initial opening balance is still outstanding
   - Outstanding: total amount owed right now
3. Green means the family is paid up or in credit. Red means money is owed.

### Submitting a Payment
1. In the Finances tab, tap the green "Submit a Payment" button.
2. Select your family from the dropdown.
3. Enter the amount (numbers only — commas are added automatically as you type).
4. Enter the bank or MoMo reference number.
5. Attach a photo of your receipt (bank screenshot or MoMo confirmation message).
6. Tap "Next" to choose how to allocate the payment: toward monthly arrears, opening balance, or future months.
7. Review the summary and tap "Confirm and Submit".
8. Hellen (Treasurer) will receive a WhatsApp notification and confirm or reject the payment.

### Using the Payment Calculator
1. In the Finances tab, find your family card.
2. Tap "Payment Calculator" on your family card.
3. Use the minus and plus buttons (or type a number) to set how many months ahead you want to pay.
4. The calculator shows: arrears + opening balance + selected future months = total to pay.
5. If you already have a credit balance, it deducts it automatically.
6. Tap "Pay UGX X →" to open the payment form pre-filled with that amount.

### Viewing Your Payment History
1. In the Finances tab, scroll to "My Submissions" near the top.
2. You will see all your payments with their status: Confirmed (green), Pending (purple), or Rejected (red).
3. If a payment was rejected, the reason is shown. Tap "Resubmit" to fix and resubmit it.
4. Tap "View receipt" on any confirmed payment to see the receipt photo you uploaded.

### Viewing Club Expenses
1. In the Finances tab, scroll down past the family balances to the Expenditure section.
2. You can filter expenses by year or category using the buttons at the top of that section.
3. Each row shows the date, description, amount, and which project it was for.
4. Admins (Hillary and Hellen) can tap "+ Add Expense" to record a new club expenditure.

### How to Record a Club Expense (Admin Only — Hillary and Hellen)
1. In the Finances tab, scroll to the Expenditure section.
2. Tap the green "+ Add Expense" button (only visible to Hillary and Hellen).
3. Fill in: the date, a clear description, the amount, the category (Project Investment, Staff, Event, Loan, Other), and optionally which project it relates to.
4. Tap "Save Expense". It will appear in the expense log immediately and update the reconciliation.

### How to Update the Bank Balance (Admin Only)
1. In the Finances tab, tap the yellow "Update balance" button next to the ABSA balance.
2. Enter the current confirmed balance from ABSA (Hellen's account statement).
3. Add a short note explaining which transactions you just confirmed.
4. Tap "Save". The reconciliation indicator updates immediately.

## How Payments Are Processed (For All Members)
- When you submit a payment, Hellen receives a WhatsApp notification.
- Hellen reviews the receipt and either confirms or rejects it.
- If confirmed: your family balance updates immediately and you receive a WhatsApp message saying "Payment Confirmed".
- If rejected: you receive a WhatsApp message with the reason. In your Finances tab, the payment shows as "Rejected" with a "Resubmit" button.
- Payments clear your oldest monthly arrears first (FIFO — oldest unpaid month cleared first).

## Projects Tab (🌾)

### Viewing All Projects
1. Tap "Projects" in the app.
2. You will see all active and planned club investments: Washing Bay, Sheep, Chicken, Dairy, Trees, Mango, Bees, etc.
3. Each project card shows its category, status, and the latest update.
4. Tap any project card to expand it and see full details, photos, and financial summaries.

### Washing Bay — Recording Monthly Income
The washing bay (Eli's business at the family compound) reports monthly revenue to the club.
1. Tap "Projects" in the app.
2. Find the "Washing Bay" project card and tap it to expand it.
3. Scroll down inside the Washing Bay card to the income section. You will see the income log and a green "Record Income" button.
4. Tap "Record Income".
5. In the modal that opens, fill in:
   - Date: the date the income was collected
   - Amount (UGX): the amount received (commas are added automatically)
   - Received From: who paid (default is "Eli")
   - Collected By: who collected the cash (default is "Dad")
   - Reference (optional): a receipt or transaction reference number
   - Notes (optional): any remarks about this collection
   - PIN: the access PIN for recording income (ask Hillary if you do not have it)
6. Tap "Save Record". The income will appear in the log immediately with a running total.
7. Note: Recording washing bay income requires the PIN. This prevents accidental entries.

### Chicken Project — Detailed Analytics
The chicken project has the most detailed tracking in the app.
1. Tap "Projects" and find the Chicken project card.
2. Tap "View Details" or the project card to open the full chicken analytics modal.
3. Inside, you will find tabs at the top. Tap each to navigate:
   - Flock: current bird count, deaths, growth, and flock events
   - Sales: revenue records and sales history
   - Financials: income vs expenses breakdown
   - Projections: financial projections for the project
   - Cash Flow: timeline of money in and money out
   - Equity Analysis: a quick link — opens the full Equity tab

### Equity Tab (standalone nav item, symbol ⚖️)
The Equity tab is a dedicated top-level section covering all three proposed expense-sharing models across the entire club (not just one project).
1. Tap "Equity" in the app (between Projects and Loans).
2. You will see:
   - A banner showing the current vote status (pending or decided at KIM 009/2026)
   - Model A: equal share per family, with gap cover for low-balance families
   - Model B: every expense split in proportion to each family's running pool balance at the date of the expense
   - Model C (Solomon's proposal): fixed percentages based on each family's monthly contribution obligation (adults x 50,000 + children x 15,000)
   - A table showing every family's current equity stake under all three models (UGX amount and % stake)
   - Per-Project Investment Share: how much each family funded each expense category
   - Full Expense Log: every recorded club expense, expandable per family per model
3. A "Download Equity Models Explainer" button at the top lets you download the full document with worked examples for all three models.
4. This section is read-only. The family vote on which model to adopt happens at a meeting.
5. The Chicken project's detail view also has an "Equity Analysis" tab that shows the same data in context.

## Updates Tab (📰)

The Updates tab shows the latest news and progress from all KimFam projects. It is updated automatically when project leads post to the family WhatsApp group.

### Viewing Updates
1. Tap the newspaper icon (Updates) in the navigation bar.
2. The most recent updates appear first, each showing the project name, author, date, and a summary.
3. If an update includes photos or videos, a media count is shown. Tap it to expand and view them.
4. Updates are pulled from the family WhatsApp group automatically.

### How Updates Are Created
- Automatic (WhatsApp): When a family member posts about a project in the KimFam WhatsApp group, the AI agent detects it, writes a clean 2-3 sentence summary, and posts it here within a few minutes.
- Manual (admin only): Hillary and Hellen can tap the Post button in the Updates tab to write a new update directly.

### Combining Messages Into One Update
If Dad sends a photo and then a caption about the same project within a few minutes, the AI clusters those messages into a single update with both text and photo. Not two separate entries.

### Editing or Removing an Update
- Admins can delete any update by tapping the trash icon on the card.
- Admins can also send "delete update <id>" via the self-chat agent command.

### Example Questions Ask KimFam Can Answer About Updates

- When did Solomon last post about the sheep?

## Troubleshooting

- **Wrong password**: Use "Forgot password?" on the login screen to reset via WhatsApp OTP.
- **Payment not updating after submission**: Pull down to refresh the Finances page using the refresh icon at the top.
- **Receipt not uploading**: The file must be a photo (JPG or PNG). Try taking a fresh screenshot.
- **WhatsApp code not arriving**: Make sure your phone number is registered with the club. If it does not arrive within 2 minutes, contact Hillary or Hellen.
- **App not loading properly**: Try clearing your browser cache or using a private/incognito window.
- **Washing Bay PIN**: If you do not know the washing bay income PIN, contact Hillary.
- **Expense record missing**: If you recorded an expense but it is not showing, it may not have saved. Try again or contact Hillary.

## Portfolio Intelligence (Projects Tab)

A Portfolio AI button appears at the top of the Projects tab. Tap it to open the Portfolio Intelligence panel, which ranks all 7 projects by strategic priority, assigns tiers (MOVE NOW, BUILD CAREFULLY, LET COMPOUND), scores each project out of 10 with rationale, gives 90-day actions, and highlights the biggest opportunity, risk, and compounding flywheel. Rankings adjust dynamically as new data enters the system.

## Project Audit Reports

Every project card has an Audit button. The Audit panel has 4 tabs: Assumptions (every input labeled ACTUAL/ESTIMATE/PROJECTION/ASSUMPTION/UNKNOWN with source and evidence), Formulas (every metric with formula and values substituted), Gaps (missing data that would improve analysis), and AI Brief (board-quality analysis with executive summary, strengths, risks, next actions, board recommendation, and surprising insight).

## Ask KimFam Audit Questions

You can ask: which project should we invest in next, what assumptions are behind the trees ROI, what is our biggest portfolio risk, what data gaps exist for dairy, how was washing bay payback calculated, what if milk prices drop.
